              How I think

              I don’t believe every SEO problem needs another blog post.

              1. Sometimes the problem is technical.
              2. Sometimes the website has the wrong architecture.
              3. Sometimes the content isn’t answering the right question.
              4. Sometimes there are hundreds of pages that shouldn’t exist.
              5. And sometimes the biggest growth opportunity is building something that doesn’t exist yet.

              I prefer understanding the problem before jumping to the tactic.

              My approach to organic growth is built around four principles.

              01

              Understand before optimizing

              I start with the data, the website, the audience and the business objective. I want to understand what is happening before deciding what needs fixing.

              02

              Think in systems

              I look for repeatable opportunities rather than one-off wins. Strategies, frameworks and processes that can be scaled sustainably.

              03

              Experiment relentlessly

              SEO isn’t static. I test, analyze, iterate and adapt. Not every experiment works, but every experiment should teach us something.

              04

              Focus on impact

              Traffic is important, but traffic alone isn’t the destination. I care about how organic growth contributes to meaningful business outcomes.
